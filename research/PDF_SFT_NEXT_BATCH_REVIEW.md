## Additional on-chain material: DP07 pages 6-14

Patrick Scott's Introduction to On-Chain Analysis includes useful distinctions between token transfers, transaction actions, token-holder addresses and attributed entities. Page 8 explicitly says not to blindly copy whale wallets. Pages 9-10 demonstrate that a transaction can involve wrapping ETH, depositing it into a protocol and receiving a receipt token; it is not necessarily a simple transfer or a buy/sell signal. Page 12 warns against mistaking labeled exchange wallets for individual whales. Page 14 distinguishes wallet size from evidence of investment skill and notes that arbitrage positions need not express a long-term directional thesis.

Suitable future questions: Why is a transfer not sufficient evidence of buying? Why should an exchange custody address not be treated as one investor? Why can a large arbitrage wallet have exposure that does not imply a bullish view? What further evidence is needed before following a tracked wallet?

## Additional technical material: DP17 pages 46-53

Trendlines visualize selected price points and depend on rules/timeframe. Pages 47-48 explain support/resistance and illustrate a support break followed by resistance. Definitions can support teaching; certainty that levels will hold cannot. Pages 50-51 distinguish indicator input types and purposes and explicitly qualify predictive claims. Page 52 introduces momentum and trading volume; page 53 connects volume to hypotheses about trend strength and introduces RSI.

Suitable future questions: How do support/resistance hypotheses differ from observed order-book depth? How do indicator inputs differ? Why does a leading indicator not guarantee a future outcome? How do momentum and volume differ? Why do volume units, venue and observation window matter?

Hold/correct: statements that higher timeframes or more trendline touches are necessarily more reliable need evidence. The claim that high volume validates a move must remain conditional and acknowledge venue/reporting quality. Pattern/indicator descriptions are not empirically verified success labels.
