# DP16 selected foundations - 23 September 2026

Staged only: eight training, four validation and four development-test records in `pdf_training_dp16_foundations.json`. This is a small selected module from *Bitcoin and Cryptocurrency Technologies*, not full-book ingestion. Author credits in the uploaded draft: Arvind Narayanan, Joseph Bonneau, Edward Felten, Andrew Miller and Steven Goldfeder; preface by Jeremy Clark. PDF page 2 dates the draft to February 9, 2016. User-attested private training permission applies; do not infer redistribution rights from free availability.

This session read pages 38-41 and 102-108 in full extracted text, plus front-matter identification. An earlier preview of pages 1-12 was truncated and is not claimed as complete review. The staged records concern signature/message binding, custody availability/security/convenience, public-ledger versus wallet-state distinctions and public/private material separation. No cryptographic implementation, key generation, address-generation formula or example secret is included.

Important exclusions: historical statements about address prefixes, all signature usage being ECDSA, simplified hierarchical derivation, external randomness requirements and brain-wallet example parameters are not treated as current complete specifications. The public-derivation privacy caveat is retained rather than asserting universal safety of disclosure.

Primary specification cross-checks: [BIP32](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0032.mediawiki) and [BIP39](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0039.mediawiki). They were consulted for scope checking, not imported wholesale. A conceptual textbook explanation is not production wallet guidance. No actual wallet, key, order or transfer operation was performed.

These evaluation records deliberately overlap the taught concepts and are development checks. Independent model evaluation and the full 309-page visual/text review remain separate work.
