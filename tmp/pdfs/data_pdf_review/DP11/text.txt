  FOR NEW COMERS | KAISE SPOT TRADING KE LIYE COIN
  SELECT KAREIN?
  STEP 1: COIN HUNTING FOR SPOT TRADING
  CoinMarketCap par jaake Top Losers dhoondhein jo 7 Days (7D) tab
  mein dikhaye gaye hain. Yeh coins woh hain jo pichle haftay mein kaafi
  price lose kar chuke hain, aur inka bounce hone ka potential zyada
  hota hai. Gain wale coins avoid karein kyunki unka momentum khatam
  ho sakta hai.
  STEPS:
1. CoinMarketCap Search:CoinMarketCap website par jaayein aur
  “Trending Coins” ko 7D Losers filter karein.
  Example: Screenshot ke mutabiq, PNUT, POPCAT, aur KAS sabse zyada
  7D mein giray hain.

2. Top 3 Losers Analyze Karein:Har coin ke baare mein research
  karein (website, use case, aur community activity check karein).
  Example: PNUT ek new meme coin hai jo animal welfare par focus
  karta hai, aur abhi price gir raha hai lekin trading volume kaafi high
   hai.

3. 1D Aur 24H Tabs:1D Tab: Dekhein ke coin ka price short-term mein
   stabilize ho raha hai ya aur gir raha hai.
  24H Tab: Agar price stable hai ya thoda sa recover kar raha hai, toh
  buying ka plan banayein.

  STEP 2: SPOT TRADING ANALYSIS ON TRADINGVIEW
  Ab coin select karne ke baad, TradingView free version par analysis
  karte hain. Yahan indicators aur support/resistance ka use karte hain
  taake signal banayein.
  SUPPORT AUR RESISTANCE KYA HAI?
 Support Level: Yeh woh price hai jahan buyers zyada hotay hain aur
  price neeche girte hue ruk jata hai. Example: PNUT ka $1.10 ek support
   level hai kyunki price wahan bounce kar raha hai.
  Resistance Level: Yeh woh price hai jahan sellers zyada hotay hain
  aur price upar jate hue wapas neeche aata hai. Example: PNUT ka
  $1.35 ek resistance hai.
  KAISE USE KAREIN?
1. Candlestick Chart Samjhein:Green candles = Price upar ja raha hai
   (bullish).
  Red candles = Price neeche ja raha hai (bearish).

=== PDF PAGE 6 ===
  Example: Agar red candles continuously neeche gir rahi hain aur ek
   level par ruk jati hain, toh wahan support ho sakta hai.

2. Support Aur Resistance Banane Ka Tareeqa:Price chart par
  dekhein ke price kin points par zyada time ruk raha hai.
  Horizontal lines draw karein wahan jahan candles wapas neeche ya
  upar jati hain.

  INDICATORS FOR SPOT TRADING:
1. Moving Averages (EMA):Add 9 EMA aur 21 EMA (free account mein
  2 EMAs add kar sakte hain).
  Logic: Jab 9 EMA (faster moving average) 21 EMA ke upar ho, toh price
   bullish hota hai (buying ka signal). Jab neeche ho, toh bearish hota hai
   (selling ka signal).

2. RSI (Relative Strength Index):RSI add karein aur dekhein ke kya
  price overbought (>70) hai ya oversold (<30).
  Logic: Jab RSI oversold hai, toh buying ka signal ho sakta hai kyunki
  price recover karega. Overbought hone par selling ka plan karein.

  STEP 3: VOLUME AUR PRICE KA RISHTA SAMAJHNA
  VOLUME KYA HAI?
  Volume dikhata hai ke kitna paisa ya kitni trades ek coin par ho rahi
   hain. Zyada volume ka matlab hai zyada activity aur price move karne
  ke chances.
  VOLUME AUR CANDLES KA RISHTA:
1. High Volume + Green Candle: Price upar jayega kyunki buyers
  zyada hain.
2. High Volume + Red Candle: Price neeche jayega kyunki sellers
  zyada hain.
3. Low Volume: Price stable ya sideways rahega.
  LOGIC:
  Jab price support level par ho aur volume zyada ho, toh buyers active
  hain aur price upar ja sakta hai.
  Jab price resistance par ho aur volume zyada ho, toh selling pressure
  aayega aur price neeche gir sakta hai.
  STEP 4: NEW COINS KE LIYE ANALYSIS KAISE KAREIN?
  Naye coins mein data limited hota hai, lekin phir bhi yeh steps use
   karein:
1. Support Aur Resistance Find Karein:Chart par short-term (15-min
  aur 1-hour) support aur resistance zones identify karein.

=== PDF PAGE 7 ===
  Example: PNUT ka $1.10 support aur $1.35 resistance hai.

2. Indicators Aur Volume Dekhein:RSI aur EMA ka use karein for trend
  confirmation.
  Volume ko candles ke saath compare karein jaise upar explain kiya
  gaya.

3. Small Trades Karein:Naye coins mein zyada risk hota hai. Pehle
  chhoti trades karein aur price movement ko samajhne ka waqt dein.

  CONCLUSION:
  Spot trading ke liye hamesha losers (7D tab) ko target karein jo ab
   stabilize ho rahe hain. CoinMarketCap aur TradingView ka logical use
  karke support/resistance, EMA, RSI aur volume ka analysis karein aur
  calculated risk lein. Indicators aur price action ke beech connection
  samajhna zaroori hai, kyunki wahi signal banane ka best tareeqa hai!

Future Trading ka Basic Concept
Socho ke tum aur tumhara dost ek cricket match dekh
rahe ho. Tumhe lagta hai Pakistan jeetega, aur tumhara
dost kehta hai doosri team jeetegi. Tum dono decide karte
ho ke jo galat hoga woh doosre ko 100 rupees dega.
Future trading mein bhi kuch aisa hi hota hai, magar
yahaan apne anadaz ke saath paisay wager (bet) ki tarah
lagate ho.
Lekin yahaan hum kisi khilaadi ke upar wager nahi lagate,
balke market ke upar apne anadaz ko trade ki form mein
lagate hain. Yani, tum yeh soch kar trade lete ho ke price
upar jayegi ya neeche aur is par tum profit bana sakte ho.
Futures trading complex hai, is mein bohot si terms aur
rules hain jo samajhne padte hain. Yahan har term ko
detail mein aise samjhaenge ke ek teenager bhi asaani se
samajh sake.
Types of Contracts in Futures Trading
1. Perpetual Contracts
• Yeh wo contracts hain jo kabhi expire nahi hote. Matlab,
tum jab tak chaho hold kar sakte ho. Lekin har kuch
ghanton mein funding fees lagti hain, jo tumhe ya tumhari
position hold karne walay doosray logon ko pay karna
padta hai.
2. Fixed-Term Contracts
• Fixed-term contracts ki ek expiry date hoti hai. Matlab,
ye contracts ek fixed time par end ho jate hain, aur us
waqt tumhara profit ya loss lock ho jata hai. Aksar log

=== PDF PAGE 9 ===
fixed-term contracts ko short-term events ke liye use karte
hain.
3. Coin-M Futures Contracts
• Coin-M contracts wo hote hain jo cryptocurrency (jaise
Bitcoin ya Ethereum) mein settle hote hain. Iska matlab
yeh hai ke agar tum profit ya loss karte ho, woh directly
cryptocurrency mein hota hai, na ke USD ya kisi aur stable
currency mein.
• Example: Agar tumne Bitcoin ke Coin-M futures par long
position li aur market tumhare favor mein gai, toh tumhara
profit Bitcoin mein add hoga. Aur agar loss ho gaya toh
woh bhi Bitcoin mein hoga.
4. USDT-M Futures Contracts
• Ismein tum USDT (Tether) use karte ho aur tumhara
profit ya loss bhi USDT mein hota hai. Iska fayda yeh hai ke
tumhara profit ya loss stable reh sakta hai aur tumhe
cryptocurrency ke fluctuation se zyada farq nahi padega.
Key Terms Aur Unki Asan Explanation
1. Leverage (10x, 50x, 100x, etc.)
• Leverage ka matlab hai ke tum apne thode paisay se
zyada badi position le sakte ho. Socho agar tumhare paas
100 rupees hain aur tum 10x leverage use karte ho, toh
tumhare paas 1000 rupees ki trading power ho jati hai. Yeh
aise hi hai ke tumne ek dost se 900 rupees uaar liye taake
bada paisa laga sako.
• Example: Tumhare paas 500 rupees hain aur tum 20x
leverage lagate ho, toh tum 10,000 rupees ki trade kar
sakte ho. Magar yaad rakho, jitna leverage badhoge, risk
bhi utna hi badh jata hai. Agar market tumhare against
chali gayi, toh tum apne poore paisay bhi lose kar sakte
ho.
2. Cross aur Isolated Margin

=== PDF PAGE 10 ===
• Cross Margin: Ismein tum apne poore wallet ko risk mein
daal dete ho. Agar trade galat jaye toh poora wallet empty
ho sakta hai.
• Isolated Margin: Tum specific trade ke liye sirf wo paisa
risk mein daalte ho jo us trade ke liye allocate kiya hai.
Baqi paisa safe rehta hai.
3. Stop Loss aur Take Profit
• Stop Loss: Tum apna loss control karne ke liye ek specific
point set kar sakte ho jahan trade close ho jata hai.
• Take Profit: Tumne agar apna desired profit achieve kar
liya, toh take profit set karke trade close kar sakte ho
taake profit lock ho jaye.
4. Market Order aur Limit Order
• Market Order: Tum instantly current price par trade karte
ho.
• Limit Order: Tum apna desired price set karte ho aur jab
market us price par pohanchti hai toh tumhara order
execute ho jata hai. Yeh tumhe apne budget mein rehte
hue trade karne mein help karta hai.
5. Long aur Short Positions
• Long Position: Tumhe lagta hai ke price upar jayegi toh
tum buy karte ho.
• Short Position: Tumhe lagta hai ke price neeche jayegi
toh tum pehle sell karte ho aur phir baad mein wapas buy
kar lete ho jab woh sasta hota hai. Yeh bilkul aise hi hai
jaise tum apne dost se kehte ho ke “mere paas yeh cheez
hai” aur jab wo cheez sasti ho jaye toh woh wapas le lete
ho.
6. Hedge Mode
• Is mode mein tum long aur short trades simultaneously
hold kar sakte ho. Yeh tumhe risk ko control mein rakhne
aur flexibility dene ke liye hota hai.
7. Funding Fee aur Funding Rate

=== PDF PAGE 11 ===
• Perpetual contracts mein funding fee lagti hai jo long aur
short position holders ke darmiyan adjust hoti hai. Funding
rate positive ho toh long wale short walon ko pay karte
hain aur vice versa.
8. Order Book aur Percentage Change (% Change)
• Order Book mein tum dekh sakte ho ke kitne log kis price
par buy ya sell karna chahte hain.
• Percentage Change tumhe batata hai ke price mein kitna
change aya hai.
9. Mark Price aur Liquidation Price
• Mark Price ek fair price hai jo exchange broader market
data ko dekh kar set karta hai.
• Liquidation Price wo point hai jahan tumhara trade
automatically close ho jata hai kyunke tumhare paas aur
loss handle karne ka balance nahi bachta.
10. Auto-Deleveraging (ADL)
• Kabhi kabhi jab market bohot volatile hoti hai toh
exchange kuch trades ko automatically close kar deta hai
taake market stable rahe. Yeh zaroori hai beginners ko
samajhne ke liye, taake woh pata rakh sakte hain ke kuch
conditions mein unka trade control se bahar ho sakta hai.
Futures Trading Mein Profits Aur Loss Kaise Hotay Hain?
Agar tum leverage ke saath trade kar rahe ho aur market
tumhare against chali gayi, toh tumhara trade liquidate ho
sakta hai. Liquidation ka matlab hai ke tumhara invested
paisa khatam ho gaya hai. Magar yaad rakho, tumhe
exchange ko aur paisay wapis nahi dene padte, sirf
tumhara invested amount khatam hota hai. Yeh risk
hamisha yaad rakho ke leverage jitna zyada hoga, risk bhi
utna hi high hoga.

 FUNDING RATE EXPLAINED WITH LOCAL EXAMPLE
 FUNDING RATE KYA HAI?
  Funding rate ek mechanism hai jo futures
  market aur spot market ke prices ko balance karne ke
  liye use hoti hai. Futures ka price kabhi kabhi spot price
  (actual market price) se zyada ya kam hota hai. Funding
  rate iss gap ko adjust karta hai.
  POSITIVE FUNDING RATE
Jab futures ka price spot price se zyada ho, toh
  funding rate positive hoti hai.
Iska matlab: Long position holders short position
  holders ko pay karte hain.
 NEGATIVE FUNDING RATE
Jab futures ka price spot price se kam ho, toh funding
  rate negative hoti hai.
Iska matlab: Short position holders long position
  holders ko pay karte hain.

1. Credit Card Aur Leverage Ka Farq
• Credit Card:
Jab tum credit card use karte ho, bank tumhe ek fixed
loan deta hai, jiska interest (sood) tumhe har mahine
dena hota hai. Agar tum repay nahi karte, to wo
compound interest (mote munafay ke sath) lagta hai, jo
haram hai.
• Example: Tumne 10,000 PKR ka loan liya, agar nahi
diya to wo tumse extra paisa vasool karenge. Ye zulm
aur haram hai.
• Leverage (Trading Exchanges par):
Exchange tumhe leverage deta hai (e.g., 10x, 20x, 50x),
jo tumhare apne funds ka multiple hota hai. Agar
tumhara trade galat jaye, to sirf tumhare initial funds
liquidate hote hain, lekin exchange tumse kuch extra
vasool nahi karta.
• Example: Agar tumhare paas $100 hain aur 10x
leverage use kiya, to tum $1,000 ka trade karte ho. Agar
price 10% neeche jaye, to tumhara $100 khatam ho
jayega, lekin exchange tumse aur paisa nahi lega.

3. Market Makers Kya Hain Aur Paisa Kaise Banate Hain?
• Market Makers bade financial institutions hote hain jo
