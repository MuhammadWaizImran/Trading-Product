## Corrections and exclusions

- Role reversal, breakouts and confirmation do not guarantee a price outcome or an executable stop.
- Absolute volume is not meaningless; distinguish relative activity from execution capacity and specify units/venue.
- Stock delivery percentages, daily opening gaps and fixed multiweek pattern durations cannot be silently transplanted into crypto.
- Page 32 mixes body engulfment with a close-above-high description and accidentally calls bullish confirmation bearish. The visually checked text was corrected to a declared body-based definition.
- Page 35's implied high reversal probability is unsupported by a measured study in the supplied material.
- ADX strength is distinct from direction; do not teach it as velocity or rely on the source's historical invention-year claim.
- Promotional pages 44-49 provide no verified training outcome labels. No discount codes, service endorsements or asserted profits are incorporated as fact.
